Use StashFlow as Your Own Standalone App (Outside Gemini)

This entire calculator is built as a self-contained application. Your data saves automatically in your browser's local storage, and you can run it on your computer or phone anytime without opening Gemini.

Option 1 • PC, Mac, or Chromebook

Double-Click & Bookmark

  1. Click Download Standalone App (.html) above to save teen_budget_calculator.html to your Desktop or Documents folder.
  2. Double-click the downloaded file — it opens directly in Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Brave like a regular app.
  3. Press Ctrl+D (or Cmd+D on Mac) to bookmark it on your bookmarks bar for 1-click access.
Option 2 • Desktop App Window

Install as a Frameless Desktop App

  1. Open the downloaded .html file in Chrome, Brave, or Microsoft Edge.
  2. Click the three-dots menu (⋮) in the top-right corner of the browser.
  3. Go to Cast, save, and share → Install page as app... (or Apps → Install this site as an app).
  4. It now gets its own icon on your Desktop/Taskbar and opens in its own standalone window!
Option 3 • iPhone / Android Home Screen

Use on Your Phone

  1. Host the file for free in 10 seconds on Netlify Drop (app.netlify.com/drop) or GitHub Pages, or open it locally on Android Chrome.
  2. On iPhone (Safari): Tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
  3. On Android (Chrome): Tap Menu (⋮) → Add to Home screen.
  4. It launches full-screen from your phone's home screen just like a native app!
